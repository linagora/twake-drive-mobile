Photo backup (Settings > Photo backup), to try on a real iPhone:
• Turning it on: pick or create the folder, then give full or limited access to the library. With limited access, a banner says so and opens Settings.
• App open: photos go, the counts move.
• App closed: take photos, come back a few hours later (faster while charging on Wi-Fi).
• In the background with many videos: a few uploads go at a time and land, with no "(1)" copies in the folder.
• Live Photos: the video then the photo reach Drive.
• Edits: an edited photo goes again as a new version; a favorite sends nothing.
• Wi-Fi only by default; try "Photos over mobile data" and "Videos over mobile data".
• "Only while charging": nothing goes on battery.
• Albums: pick one, it shows in Drive Photos.
• Move or rename the device folder in Drive: the backup goes on in it. Put it in the trash: a banner offers to make it again or turn the backup off.
• Drive full: the "Upgrade your plan" or "Information" window shows.
